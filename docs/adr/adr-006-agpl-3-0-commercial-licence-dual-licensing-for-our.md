# ADR-006: AGPL-3.0 + commercial licence (dual licensing) for our modules; contributors sign a CLA

- **Status:** Accepted (31 Oct 2026)
- **Date:** 2026-10-31

## Decision

**AGPL-3.0 + commercial licence** (dual licensing) for our modules; contributors sign a CLA

## Rationale and consequences

Open source and free for everyone, including commercial use. Anyone who modifies the code and hosts it for others must publish their changes, which protects our hosting and services revenue. Companies that won't publish changes (OEMs, integrators) can buy a commercial licence. The CLA gives us the right to relicense contributions. Lawyer to review the licence notice and CLA text
